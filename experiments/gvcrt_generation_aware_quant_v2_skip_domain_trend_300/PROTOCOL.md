# Fixed v2 skip/domain trend protocol

This directory contains a local codec subclass and a copy of the v1 training/evaluation driver adapted only for the declared 2×2 interventions and additional auditing. A and H00 use v1 A/B checkpoints and training records; they are never retrained. H10/H01/H11 each start from G_basic_100, SHA256 `89c1a5baa195c8ffc9695af495d43389e00021b926eae13a0337d3b2eb117fd4`, with exactly the H state in v1 B step 0. No V is installed. The frozen encoder, entropy models, feature decoder, bridge and reference rules are inherited unchanged.

`data_manifest.json` and `train_plan.json` are byte copies from v1. Seed 20261023, eight sources, two 16-frame windows each, 256×256 crop. The saved 300-row plan is replayed exactly, including window/QP coupling, order, crop and frame offsets. I initialization; P1–11 no-gradient warmup; P12–15 each contribute 1/4 loss. Every next-frame reference is detached. Quantization may naturally change subsequent references. External QP 2/7; native INDEX_MAP offsets. `effective_runtime_audit.json` records INDEX_MAP=[0,1,0,2,0,2,0,2], qp_shift=[0,2,1], and effective eight-frame offsets=[0,2,0,1,0,1,0,1]. The inherited v1 config’s four-entry descriptive offset field is retained, and this runtime audit gives the complete executed sequence.

Objective: LPIPS + 0.1383589248954108 × L1 + beta_q(schedule_s1p0, external_QP) × estimated_bpp. AdamW G=1e-6, H=1e-4, betas=(0.9,0.999), eps=1e-8, weight_decay=1e-4, global clip=1.0. Frozen codec remains FP16; G/H parameters and accumulated gradients remain FP32 as in v1. Exactly 300 effective updates, snapshots 0/100/300, per-update optimizer/RNG/cursor checkpoint. No loss search, dataset enlargement, epoch extension or selective early stopping.

H10/H11 multiply the original symbol STE by the active mask. Mean restoration and final multiplication by q retain their gradients. Active-symbol STE, skip threshold .12, symbol clamp [-128,127], masks and hard forward remain unchanged.

H01/H11 use q_base after native lower clamp and q_new after H adjustment, lower clamp and dtype conversion; rho=q_new/q_base in the actual codec dtype. First-pass means/scales divide by rho. Decoded first-pass u0_new multiplies by rho before concatenating with the original unchanged common parameters. Second-pass output means/scales divide by rho. Reconstruction multiplies by q_new. `compress_prior_2x`, both decompression parts and differentiable training use this definition. The bitstream container, two-pass order and available decoder conditions are unchanged. This is a deterministic reparameterization test, without a calibration claim.

Preflight must pass before training. Fixed tolerances are stored in `preflight.py` and `preflight.json`; symbol/q/decode-state comparisons are exact; training-vs-codec latent/feature absolute tolerance .01 accounts for native half precision; identity tolerance 1e-5. CPU skipped-state finite difference uses epsilon 1e-5, away from thresholds, with expected corrected dy_hat/dq=.5. Ordinary discrete round is not tested against an STE finite difference. Synthetic tests cover nonidentity multipliers, actual-rho deviation at clamp, skip/non-skip and both signed symbol saturation endpoints in both passes. Persisted P packets are decoded by fresh models, and full evaluation uses persisted native containers.

GPU assignment: H10=physical4, H01=5, H11=6, preflight/evaluation=7. GPU locks serialize this experiment and occupancy polling queues behind other jobs without terminating them. CPU orchestration uses no additional GPU. Each group has a separate training CSV, merged only after all finish.

Evaluation: 64 frames UVal0/UVal1 at updates 0/100/300 and external QP2/7; step300 also ParkScene/Jockey. New measurements instantiate fresh decoders reading persisted files; all64 encoder/decoder causal references must agree exactly. New tasks also check every P latent/qmap. Eligible v1 results are explicitly marked reused with source/checkpoint/stream/table SHA; old tasks lacking all64 reference assertions are remeasured. Metrics retain v1 frame_metrics and FloLPIPS implementations. Exact total bytes, P payload and headers are separated; analytical estimated y/z bits are compared with actual P payload over P1–63. Actual interleaved RANS y/z bytes are not separately attributed.

Per-frame and transition tables are retained. Segment summaries cover frame indices 0–14 and 15–63; P-only segment payload omits frame0. Fixed previews use frame15, rows200:456, columns400:656, step300 and QP2 for every method/video. No image/region is selected by outcome.

Matched-rate comparisons use actual total bpp; interpolate only inside measured overlapping intervals. Supplemental QPs are selected only from actual rate endpoints, external legal range0–9, at most three per method/video. Decisions and rate-only evidence are saved before running them. No extrapolation and no formal BD-rate claim. Curves and differences are descriptive: 300 updates do not establish convergence, statistical significance, or strictly unseen-data generalization; historical G_basic_100 exposure is not fully established.
